# How the Country Scoring Works
### A Plain-English Guide for HopeChest Staff

---

## The Short Version

Every church that fills out the fit guide gets a hidden point score calculated for each of the three countries — Guatemala, Uganda, and Ethiopia. The country with the most points becomes the #1 recommendation, the next becomes #2, and so on. The guide then converts those raw points into the "% Match" percentages shown in the email.

---

## Starting Point

All three countries begin tied at **50 points each**. Everything after that is added or subtracted based on how the church answers each question.

---

## Question-by-Question Breakdown

### 1. Church Attendance

This is the first question and it immediately favors Guatemala for smaller churches.

| Attendance | Points Added |
|---|---|
| Under 50 | Guatemala +20 |
| 50–125 | Guatemala +20 |
| 125–300 | Guatemala +18 |
| 300–500 | Guatemala +10 |
| 500+ | No change for anyone |

**Why:** Guatemala is considered the most accessible "first trip" for smaller churches. Larger churches are seen as equally ready for any of the three countries.

---

### 2. Existing Global Presence

This question asks whether the church already does international ministry.

**If they answer "Not sure":** All three countries get +2 (effectively a wash — no meaningful change).

**If they answer "No" (no existing global presence):** No points change.

**If they answer "Yes, we already have global partnerships":**

- *And they want to go somewhere different from where they already work:*
  - Uganda +8, Ethiopia +8
  - **And** if they mention they already work in **Latin America** (Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, etc.): Guatemala −50 (a heavy penalty to steer them away), Uganda +6, Ethiopia +6
  - **And** if they mention they already work in **Africa**: Uganda −50, Ethiopia −50, Guatemala +10 (same heavy penalty in the other direction)

- *And they're "not sure" where they want to go:* Guatemala +3

**Why the −50 penalties exist:** The logic is that churches shouldn't be recommended a country they already work in. If they already have Latin America partnerships and want something new, Guatemala gets pushed way down the list.

---

### 3. How Important Is Trip Cost?

Guatemala is the least expensive option ($1,500–$2,200 vs. $1,800–$2,750 for the others).

| Answer | Points Added |
|---|---|
| Very Important | Guatemala +30 |
| Somewhat Important | Guatemala +15 |
| Not Important | No change for anyone |

---

### 4. How Important Is Time Away?

Guatemala trips are 5–6 days. Uganda and Ethiopia trips are ~10 days. Ethiopia has some direct U.S. flights that reduce total travel time.

| Answer | Points Added |
|---|---|
| Very Important | Guatemala +25, Ethiopia +10, Uganda **−5** |
| Somewhat Important | Guatemala +12, Ethiopia +6, Uganda **−3** |
| Not Important | No change for anyone |

**Note:** Uganda is the only country that can actually *lose* points — it gets penalized when time away is a concern, because it typically requires more flight connections.

---

### 5. How Important Is English Being Spoken?

Uganda is the only country of the three where English is the official language.

| Answer | Points Added |
|---|---|
| Very Important | Uganda +30 |
| Somewhat Important | Uganda +15 |
| Not Important | No change for anyone |

---

### 6. Partnership Posture

This question asks how the church wants to structure their relationship with the community.

| Answer | Points Added |
|---|---|
| "We want to own our own community" | No score change |
| "We prefer to partner alongside others" | Uganda +8, Ethiopia +8 |
| "We're flexible" | All three +3 (a wash) |
| "Not sure" | No score change |

**Note:** If "Not sure" is selected and Guatemala is already tied with another country, Guatemala gets a small tiebreaker boost (+3) in the tie-breaking phase. The reasoning: Guatemala has a lower barrier to travel (shorter trip, lower cost), so it's a reasonable default when a church hasn't landed on a partnership direction yet.

---

### 7. Who Do You Want to Mobilize? (Multi-select)

For each option the church selects, points are added:

| Selection | Points Added |
|---|---|
| Adults / Seniors | Guatemala +2 |
| Broad church-wide involvement | Guatemala +3 |
| Families with children | All three +2 |
| Construction teams | All three +2 |
| Medical professionals | All three +2 |
| Any other option | All three +2 |

**Note:** "Broad church-wide" is the only mobilization option that exclusively boosts one country (Guatemala). All others either boost Guatemala only slightly more, or treat all countries equally.

---

### 8. Mobilization "Other" (Free-text field)

If the church types anything into the open-ended "other" field, the system scans it for keywords:

| Keywords Detected | Points Added |
|---|---|
| "Spanish," "Hispanic," "Latino," "Latin America," "Central America," "Guatemala," "coffee" | Guatemala +10 |
| "Uganda," "Ugandan," "East Africa," "African," "Africa," "Safari," "Swahili" | Uganda +10 |
| "Ethiopia," "Ethiopian," "Muslim," "Horn of Africa," "Africa," "Amharic" | Ethiopia +10 |
| "Africa" or "African" (without specifying Uganda or Ethiopia) | Uganda +5 AND Ethiopia +5 |

---

### 9. Impact DNA (What Kind of Ministry Excites You?)

Churches can select multiple options. Each selection runs through the scoring once.

| Selection | Points Added |
|---|---|
| Frontier / hard-to-reach peoples | Uganda +15, Ethiopia +10 |
| — Sub-type: minimal infrastructure | Uganda +10 more |
| — Sub-type: Muslim-majority context | Ethiopia +10 more |
| — Sub-type: "Not sure" | Uganda +2, Ethiopia +2 more |
| Evangelism & discipleship | All three +5 |
| Church planting | Uganda +8, Ethiopia +8, Guatemala +3 |
| Education & medical | All three +5 |
| Community transformation, youth development, or anything else | All three +5 |

---

## Tie-Breaking (Applied Only When Two Countries Are Within 5 Points)

If the top two countries are very close, one small additional rule applies:

- **Uganda vs. Ethiopia, and English is "very" or "somewhat" important** → Uganda +3
- **Uganda vs. Ethiopia, and time away is "very" or "somewhat" important** → Ethiopia +3
- **Guatemala is in the top two, and Partnership Posture is "Not sure"** → Guatemala +3
- **Guatemala is in the top two, and the "Other" text field contains "Spanish," "Español," or "Hispanic"** → Guatemala +3

These only fire when there is a near-tie. They have no effect when one country is clearly ahead.

---

## How the Displayed Percentages Are Calculated

The raw point totals are never shown directly. Instead, they get converted to the percentages shown in the email:

- **#1 country always displays as 100%** — regardless of its actual score
- **#2 and #3 are calculated relative to #1:**
  `Displayed % = 85 + (their score ÷ #1's score × 15)`

This means the displayed percentages are always between **85% and 100%**, no matter how close or far apart the countries actually are. A country that barely squeaked into second place will still show at least 85%.

**Practical implication:** The percentages communicate relative ranking and confidence, not an absolute measure of fit. Even the "worst" match among the top 3 always looks like a strong recommendation.

---

## Summary: What Drives Guatemala to the Top?

Guatemala has built-in advantages on four of the highest-weighted questions:

| Factor | Max Guatemala Bonus |
|---|---|
| Small church attendance | +20 |
| Cost is important | +30 |
| Time away is important | +25 |
| "Broad church-wide" mobilization | +3 |
| **Total potential bonus** | **+78** |

Uganda's main advantage is English (+30). Ethiopia's main advantage is for time-sensitive churches that also care about frontier ministry. Neither African country benefits from attendance size.

**The result:** Any church that is small, cost-conscious, or time-conscious will almost always see Guatemala as #1, regardless of their mission interests. This may or may not reflect the right outcome and is worth reviewing if the client feels Guatemala is being over-recommended.

---

## Things That Have No Effect on Scoring

- Church name, denomination, contact name, contact role, or email
- The "confidence" level shown in reports (this is a separate calculation that does not affect country rankings — it only reflects how definitive the answers were)
